title: Payment lifecycle
description: A claim remains unverified until an authorized trusted review confirms it.
---

## The state machine

~~~text
AWAITING_PAYMENT
  ├─ customer submits claim ───────► PAID_UNVERIFIED
  ├─ authorized cancellation ──────► CANCELLED
  └─ deadline passes ──────────────► EXPIRED

PAID_UNVERIFIED
  ├─ authorized trusted approval ──► PAID
  └─ authorized rejection ─────────► AWAITING_PAYMENT or EXPIRED
~~~

<code>PAID</code>, <code>EXPIRED</code>, and <code>CANCELLED</code> are terminal states in the current API.

## What each state means

| State | Meaning | Fulfillment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <code>AWAITING_PAYMENT</code> | The intent is open and the customer can transfer. | Blocked |
| <code>PAID_UNVERIFIED</code> | A customer claim was received; settlement is not confirmed. | Blocked |
| <code>PAID</code> | An authorized reviewer approved the claim using a trusted source. | May proceed after the committed outbox event |
| <code>EXPIRED</code> | The payment window ended without an active claim. | Blocked |
| <code>CANCELLED</code> | An authorized actor cancelled the intent. | Blocked |

## Safe sequence

1. Your server creates the intent with a trusted amount, merchant, order, method, and idempotency key.
2. Your app displays merchant-approved manual transfer instructions.
3. A customer claim moves the intent to <code>PAID_UNVERIFIED</code>.
4. A reviewer checks a source the merchant trusts outside the customer-facing flow.
5. The privileged verification operation records who approved, what source they checked, and when.
6. In the same transaction, the intent becomes <code>PAID</code> and the <code>payment.paid</code> outbox event is stored.
7. Your fulfillment consumer handles that event after commit and deduplicates by event ID.

A screenshot, a customer-entered reference, or a “paid” button is not trusted proof. A report must never trigger fulfillment or emit <code>payment.paid</code>.

## Rejection and expiry

Rejecting a claim before the intent deadline returns it to <code>AWAITING_PAYMENT</code>. Rejecting it after the deadline moves the intent to <code>EXPIRED</code>. An active <code>PAID_UNVERIFIED</code> claim remains reviewable after the deadline; expiration does not discard it.

## Amounts

Store EGP as integer piasters. Do not convert to floating-point pounds for comparisons or storage. The host application calculates the trusted order total and passes it to the core.
