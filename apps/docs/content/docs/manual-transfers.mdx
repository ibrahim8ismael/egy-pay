## Supported methods

The current domain accepts <code>vodafone_cash</code> and <code>instapay</code>. They represent manual transfer workflows. egy-pay does not create provider checkout sessions or move funds.

Your application owns the recipient instructions: wallet or account details, labels, customer-facing copy, and any merchant-specific transfer steps. Load those values from trusted server-side configuration and display them to the customer.

## Customer experience

1. Your backend creates a payment intent from the trusted order amount.
2. Your app shows the configured transfer instructions for the selected method.
3. The customer makes the transfer in their own wallet or banking app.
4. The customer submits a claim in your application.
5. Your app shows that the claim is awaiting review and keeps fulfillment blocked.
6. A merchant reviewer checks a trusted transaction source.

Do not ask customers to submit wallet credentials, banking passwords, SMS messages, or payment-card data. Keep uploaded evidence in a separately access-controlled store and pass only an evidence reference to egy-pay.

## No unofficial integrations

There is no bundled Vodafone Cash or InstaPay network connector. Do not rely on reverse-engineered APIs, scrape consumer applications, read SMS inboxes, or infer settlement from screenshots. Add automatic verification only when the merchant has an official, authorized, and tested transaction source.
