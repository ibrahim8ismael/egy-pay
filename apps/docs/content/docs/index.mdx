egy-pay is a server-side TypeScript library for recording manual payment workflows. It gives your application a durable lifecycle for a transfer claim, review, and confirmed payment.

The central rule is simple: a customer report is not settlement proof.

## What the library handles

- Payment intents tied to your application’s order IDs.
- Customer claims recorded as <code>PAID_UNVERIFIED</code>.
- Authorized verification that records provenance before moving an intent to <code>PAID</code>.
- A transactional storage contract, idempotent commands, an audit trail, and an outbox.
- Conservative matching of trusted transaction rows for review.

## What your application owns

Your host app authenticates users, authorizes merchant and order access, calculates the amount, and displays the transfer instructions. It also decides how confirmed payment events trigger fulfillment.
